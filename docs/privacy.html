Pocket Pet privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Pocket Pet is a free, open-source desktop pet for macOS. It runs entirely on your Mac. There is no Pocket Pet server and no Pocket Pet account, and the app does not collect analytics.

What Pocket Pet reads from your Google account

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Pocket Pet asks for:

  • Your email address (openid, email), only to show which account is connected.
  • Read-only access to your calendars (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly), so your pet can show today's events, remind you before they start, and list what's due soon.

Pocket Pet never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where your data goes

Your Mac talks to Google directly. Calendar events are kept in memory while the app runs and are not saved to disk. The sign-in token is saved on your Mac in a file only your macOS account can read, encrypted with a key unique to that Mac. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Pocket Pet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show your own calendar to you in the app, is not transferred to anyone, is not used to train AI or machine learning models, and is not read by any person.

Removing access

Choose "Sign out" in Pocket Pet's menu to delete the saved token from your Mac. You can also remove Pocket Pet's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other connections

Calendar links you paste (for example from ManageBac) and your Mac's own calendars are read in the same way: on your Mac only. Music features talk only to the music app on your Mac, or to Spotify if you connect it yourself.

To check for updates, Pocket Pet asks GitHub for its latest release. That request carries no information about you beyond what any web request does.
